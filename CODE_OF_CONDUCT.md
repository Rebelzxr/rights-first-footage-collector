# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate credential exposure and knowingly submitting unlicensed media are not accepted. Discuss the work, not the person. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict repeat offenders.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact options rather than escalating them in public threads.
